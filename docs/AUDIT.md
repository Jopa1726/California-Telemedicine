# Audit: Current site + California competitors

**Prepared:** 2026-10-09. **Method:** Public search-index snapshots and official pages
(the sandbox could not load pages directly; findings come from indexed titles/snippets
and primary `.gov` sources). Each claim is dated and linked. **Verified observations**
are separated from **recommendations**. Nothing here reproduces competitor wording,
layout, testimonials, or medical claims.

> Treat competitor "gaps" as *not observed on the pages indexed*, not proof a feature
> is absent. Where we recommend "do X better," we mean do it clearly and verifiably —
> not that a competitor fails to do it.

## 1. Current site — Doctors of Natural Medicine (drnatmed.com)

### Verified observations (with sources)
- **Brand & focus.** "Doctors of Natural Medicine – Medical Marijuana Doctor… connecting patients to qualified physicians evaluating them for a Colorado medical MMJ card." — [drnatmed.com home](https://drnatmed.com/) (indexed 2026).
- **Clinics:** Colorado Springs (flagship, "the Clinic that started providing red cards in Colorado Springs… In 2016"), Lakewood/Denver ("200 Union Blvd., Ste. #200, Lakewood, CO 80228"), Fort Collins, Pueblo ("635 West Corona Ave."). — [locations](https://drnatmed.com/locations/), [Colorado Springs](https://drnatmed.com/colorado-springs/), [Denver/Lakewood](https://drnatmed.com/medical-marijuana-doctor-denver/), [Pueblo](https://drnatmed.com/pueblo/).
- **Phone:** (719) 212-8899. — [contact](https://drnatmed.com/contact/).
- **Existing telemedicine:** Phone-based tele-medicine in Colorado, expanded during COVID. — [remote locations](https://drnatmed.com/remote-locations/).
- **Appointment journey:** Online appointment request + prepay discount ("code SAVE20… $20 off"). "All patients under 21… require two appointments with two individual doctors." "Colorado residents only (You must have a valid COLORADO ID…)." — [appointment](https://drnatmed.com/appointment/).
- **Policies exist:** cancellation/reschedule ("$20 rescheduling fee"), a guarantee policy, and a cannabis evaluation consent form establishing the provider-patient relationship. — [guarantee policy](https://drnatmed.com/guarantee-policy/), [consent](https://drnatmed.com/consent-for-mmj/).
- **Content/SEO assets:** location pages, FAQ, renewal + application how-tos, registration tutorials, reviews page, dispensary partners, and blog posts (incl. older out-of-state posts e.g. AZ/NY renewals). — [faq](https://drnatmed.com/faq/), [renewal](https://drnatmed.com/medical-marijuana-card-renewal-colorado/), [apply](https://drnatmed.com/apply-mmj-card-colorado/), [tutorials](https://drnatmed.com/tutorial-for-registration/).

### Conflicting / caution-worthy claims on the live site (flagged, not "resolved")
These must be reconciled by the business with real records before reuse. **Do not pick the most persuasive number.**
- **Review volume/rating:** home page states "4.9 stars… over 2,000 reviews on Google." — [home](https://drnatmed.com/). *Needs a dated source of truth; label as Colorado reviews only.*
- **Annual evaluations:** home page states "over 9,000 evaluations per year." — [home](https://drnatmed.com/). *Verify against internal data; it's a strong claim.*
- **Tenure:** "In 2016 we set ourselves out on a mission" (Colorado Springs) implies founding ~2016. *Confirm the authoritative founding year; some clinic pages may imply different timelines. Use only to describe Colorado history — never to imply California tenure.*
- **Superlatives:** "premier," "best prices," "least expensive clinic," "price match and beat." — various location pages. *These are Colorado marketing claims; do NOT port superlatives like "best/#1" into California, and do not make price claims we can't substantiate.*
- **Terminology:** the "about" page calls it "a cannabis prescription" in one place. — [about](https://drnatmed.com/about-doctors-of-natural-medicine/). *A recommendation is NOT a prescription; the California site must avoid this wording.*

### Recommendations (current site → California)
1. Keep Colorado pages and URLs intact; add California as a sibling section (`/california/`), not a replacement.
2. Reuse the credible *brand history* and *review reputation* as **trust context**, clearly labeled as Colorado.
3. Do not carry over Colorado-specific rules (resident-only, "red card," under-21 two-doctor rule, state registry steps) onto California pages — California access works differently.

### Verified competitor patterns (not opinions)
- All three center a **single, fast online flow** and a **"money-back if not approved"** promise.
- All three publish **transparent, low headline prices** on the California page.
- **Leafwell** publishes **Spanish-language** California pages — a real localization advantage.
- All three separate **marketing content** from the **clinical/account flow**.

### Where our site can serve patients better (≥5 concrete, verifiable)
1. **Radically clear "recommendation vs. private card vs. government MMIC"** explainer with linked `.gov` sources — reduces the single most common point of confusion. (Built: homepage §E + Resources.)
2. **No "guaranteed approval" language.** We state plainly that approval depends on the physician's evaluation, which is both compliant and more trustworthy to a cautious first-timer. (Built across site; competitors lean on "money-back guarantee" framing.)
3. **Honest, source-cited cost picture** that separates *our fee* from the *optional county MMIC fee* (and names the up-to-$100 / 50%-Medi-Cal cap) — most pages blur these. (Built: Pricing + Resources.)
4. **Visible state selector + pre-booking "where are you?" router** so Colorado and California patients never land in the wrong flow, and physicians have an obvious path. (Built: header + How-it-works router.)
5. **Established multi-clinic brand credibility** (since 2016; four Colorado clinics; large Colorado review base) presented *honestly as Colorado context* — a trust signal pure-telehealth startups can't claim. (Built: Trust strip, clearly labeled.)
6. **Balanced risk/interaction education** (impairment, drug interactions, pregnancy, "natural ≠ risk-free") rather than benefits-only copy. (Built: Resources + FAQ.)
7. **Accessibility to WCAG 2.2 AA** and a fully JS-free-readable experience — not assumed of competitor funnels. (Built + QA'd.)
8. **Spanish-language parity (planned).** Match Leafwell's localization as a fast-follow (documented in SEO plan) rather than ignore it.
