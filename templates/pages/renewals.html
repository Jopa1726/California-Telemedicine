California / Renewals

Renewing your California recommendation

A straightforward renewal with a California‑licensed physician — whether or not you got your original recommendation from us.

Switching to us is fine

You don’t have to use the same provider every time. If your California recommendation came from somewhere else, you’re welcome to renew with one of our California‑licensed physicians.

A real review, every time

A compliant renewal isn’t just a fee you pay to keep an old letter alive. A California‑licensed physician reviews your current situation so your recommendation reflects an up‑to‑date evaluation.

What to have ready

  • A valid government‑issued photo ID.
  • Your current or recently expired recommendation, if you have it, so the physician can review your prior approval.
  • A private space and a device with a camera — you must be physically located in California during the visit.

You’ll upload any documents inside the secure clinical system during intake — never through a public web form or email.

Timing tip

Renewing a little before your current recommendation lapses helps you avoid a gap. Renewal cost is shown on our pricing page.
