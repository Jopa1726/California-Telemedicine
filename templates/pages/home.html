Understanding California access

Recommendation vs. patient card vs. the government MMIC

These three things are easy to confuse. Here’s the honest version.

What we help with

Physician’s recommendation

A California‑licensed physician’s written recommendation that medical cannabis may be appropriate for you, based on an evaluation. It is not a conventional prescription.

Optional

A private patient ID card

Some services offer an optional private patient ID card for convenience. It is not a government document and is different from the MMIC below.

Government · county‑run

The voluntary state MMIC

The Medical Marijuana Identification Card is a voluntary card from your county program under the California Department of Public Health. We don’t issue it. It can reduce cannabis taxes and is honored statewide.

Questions patients ask

A few quick answers

Who may be eligible?
California law lets a physician recommend cannabis when, in their judgment, it may help a patient’s serious medical condition. Eligibility is decided case‑by‑case by the physician — not by a checklist.
Is approval guaranteed?
No. The physician must independently determine that medical cannabis is appropriate for you. We never guarantee approval, and we don’t offer “guaranteed approval” promises.
Where must I be during the appointment?
You must be physically located in California at the time of your telemedicine evaluation with a California‑licensed physician. You’ll confirm this in the secure clinical workflow.
What if the physician doesn’t recommend cannabis?
They’ll explain their reasoning and may suggest other options or that you follow up with your regular provider. A medical cannabis recommendation isn’t right for everyone.
