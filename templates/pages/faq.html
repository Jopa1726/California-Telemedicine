California evaluation FAQ

Honest answers to the questions patients ask most. When something depends on the physician or your county, we say so.

What happens during an evaluation?
You meet a California‑licensed physician by secure video. They review your intake information, talk with you about your health and goals, answer your questions, and decide whether a medical cannabis recommendation is appropriate. It’s a real clinical conversation, not a form you fill out.
Who may be eligible?
California law allows a physician to recommend cannabis when, in their professional judgment, a patient’s health may benefit from it for a serious medical condition. There’s no fixed checklist — the physician decides individually based on your situation.
Is approval guaranteed?
No. Approval always depends on the physician’s independent evaluation. We do not guarantee approval and we don’t offer “guaranteed approval.” Cannabis isn’t appropriate for everyone.
What documents should I prepare?
Have a valid government‑issued photo ID ready. If you’re renewing, your current or recently expired recommendation is helpful. Any documents are uploaded inside the secure clinical system during intake — never through a public web form or email.
Where must I be located during the consultation?
You must be physically located in California at the time of your telemedicine visit, because you’re being evaluated by a California‑licensed physician. You’ll confirm your location in the secure clinical workflow.

What happens if the physician does not recommend cannabis?
The physician will explain their reasoning and may suggest other options or that you follow up with your regular provider. A recommendation isn’t right for every patient, and part of good care is saying so.
What’s the difference between a recommendation and the government MMIC?
A physician’s recommendation is a clinical document saying medical cannabis may be appropriate for you — it is not a prescription. The MMIC (Medical Marijuana Identification Card) is a separate, voluntary card issued by your county program under the California Department of Public Health. We help with the evaluation; we do not issue the government MMIC. See our resources for a full explainer.
How do renewals work?
A California‑licensed physician reviews your current situation and, if appropriate, renews your recommendation. You can renew with us even if your original recommendation came from another provider. See renewals.
How is my information handled?
Public website forms collect only minimal contact details. Medical history, documents, and consent live only in an approved secure clinical system. We do not send sensitive information through ordinary email or website analytics, and we don’t use advertising pixels or session replay in clinical flows. See our privacy information.

Why might someone consider a medical evaluation in a state with adult‑use cannabis?
Even where adult‑use cannabis is legal, some patients choose a medical pathway for reasons such as a physician’s guidance tailored to their condition, access to the voluntary county MMIC (which can reduce certain cannabis taxes), and documentation that may matter in specific situations. Whether any of this applies to you is a personal and medical decision — and the tax benefit specifically comes from the county MMIC, not from the recommendation alone.
This FAQ is general education, not medical or legal advice. Cannabis can cause side effects, interact with medications, and impair driving. “Natural” does not mean risk‑free. Talk with a physician about your specific situation.
