The county MMIC fee is separate — and optional

Separate from our fee: the optional county MMIC costs up to $100 per application/renewal (50% off for Medi-Cal), set and collected by your county — not by us.

  • Our evaluation fee covers the physician consultation and recommendation. The MMIC is a different thing you may choose to pursue.
  • The MMIC is applied for through your county program and paid to the county — not to us.
  • County MMIC fees are capped by state rule at up to $100 per application or renewal, with a 50% reduction for Medi‑Cal beneficiaries. Exact amounts vary by county.

County fee figures per California Department of Public Health MMIC program rules (see Resources for sources).

Important: A physician’s medical cannabis recommendation is not a conventional prescription and is not the government Medical Marijuana Identification Card (MMIC). The MMIC is a voluntary, county‑administered card issued by county programs under the California Department of Public Health — we do not issue it. Approval always depends on an individual physician’s evaluation; cannabis is not appropriate for everyone, and “natural” does not mean risk‑free.
