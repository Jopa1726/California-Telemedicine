California / How it works

How online California evaluations work

A clear look at every step — and an honest explanation of what the physician, not the website, decides.

Step 1

Tell us where you are, then choose a time

Because a California patient must be evaluated by a California‑licensed physician, the first thing we ask is your state. From there you choose an appointment time that fits your schedule, shown in your local time zone.

Step 2

Complete secure intake and meet your physician

You’ll complete intake inside a secure clinical system — not an ordinary web form. During your video visit, a California‑licensed physician reviews your information, discusses your health, and confirms you are physically located in California.

Your privacy matters. Medical history, documents, and consent live only in the approved secure clinical system. We never collect that information through public website forms, email, or analytics.
Step 3

If appropriate, receive a recommendation and next steps

If the physician determines medical cannabis may be appropriate for you, you’ll receive a recommendation and clear guidance on what to do next — including how the optional, county‑issued MMIC works if you want it. If cannabis isn’t appropriate, the physician will explain why.

What approval depends on. A recommendation is a medical decision based on the physician’s evaluation. It is not guaranteed, it is not a conventional prescription, and it is not the government MMIC.
