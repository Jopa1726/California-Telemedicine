Important: A physician’s medical cannabis recommendation is not a conventional prescription and is not the government Medical Marijuana Identification Card (MMIC). The MMIC is a voluntary, county‑administered card issued by county programs under the California Department of Public Health — we do not issue it. Approval always depends on an individual physician’s evaluation; cannabis is not appropriate for everyone, and “natural” does not mean risk‑free.
