California / Physicians

The physicians who evaluate you

California patients are evaluated only by physicians who hold a current California license. Here’s our standard — and how you can verify it.

Our standard

California‑licensed, always

Under California rules, a physician may recommend cannabis to a California patient only if they are that patient’s attending physician and hold a current California medical license — the standard of care is the same whether a visit happens in person or by telehealth. We will not present a physician licensed only in another state as a California clinician.

  • Each California physician holds a current California MD or DO license.
  • Clinical judgment is independent of marketing or booking targets.
  • We publish real names, approved photos, and a license‑verification link for each physician as they join.
Verify any California license yourself. You can look up a physician’s license status on the California Department of Consumer Affairs license search.

Important: A physician’s medical cannabis recommendation is not a conventional prescription and is not the government Medical Marijuana Identification Card (MMIC). The MMIC is a voluntary, county‑administered card issued by county programs under the California Department of Public Health — we do not issue it. Approval always depends on an individual physician’s evaluation; cannabis is not appropriate for everyone, and “natural” does not mean risk‑free.
